Между абзацами — короткая пауза, они же точки склейки кадров.

Did ancient humans believe in God?

Travel back fifty thousand years. Ask them.

No temples. No holy books. No priests. No religions.

Yet archaeologists keep finding something that shouldn't exist.

Imagine standing alone at night, a hundred thousand years ago.

No science. No explanations. The world feels alive, watching, conscious.

The river has a spirit. The forest. The mountain. The wind.

Our brains evolved to sense someone hiding in the dark.

Then humans understood something terrifying. Everyone dies.

They buried their dead with tools, pigments, jewelry. Why?

They painted sacred animals deep inside caves nobody ever lived in.

Religion didn't create the search for meaning. The search came first.

Today we map galaxies, DNA, the human brain.

And we're still asking the same questions.
